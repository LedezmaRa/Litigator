Final Word

The market is pricing a "Goldilocks recession" — just enough economic weakness to bring inflation down without triggering systemic stress. But the data suggests something more dangerous: stagflation lite. Energy-driven inflation persists while credit conditions tighten and growth slows. In this environment, own what benefits from dollar weakness and commodity strength (Energy, Materials, Industrials, Staples) while avoiding rate-sensitive sectors (REITs, Financials, Consumer Discretionary). The winners will be those who recognize that traditional monetary policy tools are increasingly ineffective against supply-side inflation shocks.
